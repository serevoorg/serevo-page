Privacy Policy

Effective: October 6, 2026 · Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Serevo app ("Serevo", "we", "us") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. Serevo is operated by its developer; contact us at support@serevo.org.

What Serevo is, and is not

Serevo is a wellness and coaching tool. It is not therapy, medical advice or an emergency service. The chat is an AI, not a person or a therapist. If you are in danger or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency number. findahelpline.com lists free helplines by country.

Who can use Serevo

Serevo is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a person under 18 has used Serevo, contact us and we will delete their account.

Information we collect

  • Account: email address, name, optional profile picture, and how you sign in (email code, Google or Facebook).
  • What you add: moods and mood notes, journal entries, daily intentions and obstacles, chat messages, short summaries of past chats that help the chat remember you, activity and progress data.
  • Subscription: whether you have a trial or Premium. Payments are handled by Google Play; we do not see your card details.
  • Error reports: when the app has a problem, we record the screen, error message, app version and device platform, linked to your account, so we can fix bugs.
  • On your device: settings and reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; we do not send push notifications from a server.

Moods, journal entries and chat messages can be sensitive. We use them only to run your account and the features you choose, never for advertising.

How we use it

  • To provide the app: sign-in, saving your entries, showing your history.
  • To give chat and journal replies and to remember your past chats.
  • To send the sign-in code and essential service messages.
  • To fix bugs, keep the service secure, and prevent abuse.
  • To manage your trial and subscription.

The AI chat

Your chat and journal messages are sent to Anthropic, our AI provider (United States), to write replies. Anthropic's commercial terms say it does not train its models on this content. Anthropic deletes API inputs and outputs within about 30 days, and may keep them longer if a message is flagged for misuse or the law requires it. We also store your chat history and summaries in our own database so the chat can continue where you left off. You can delete them by deleting your account.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information and we do not show ads. We use these service providers:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and the emailed sign-in code. Our database is hosted in Mumbai, India.
  • Anthropic: the AI chat and journal replies (United States).
  • RevenueCat and Google Play: subscriptions. RevenueCat keeps a customer record that is not removed when you delete your account.
  • Google or Facebook, only if you choose to sign in with them. They tell us your name and email. We have turned off advertising ID collection and automatic event logging for the Facebook sign-in tool.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect people's safety.

International transfers

Your information is processed in India and the United States. We rely on our providers' standard contractual safeguards for these transfers.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account exists. In the app, go to Settings, then Data and Privacy, to export your data (JSON) or delete your account. Deleting your account removes your account and the data linked to it from our database and wipes the app's data on your phone. Backups kept by our hosting provider and Anthropic's short retention period may keep copies for a limited time. Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription: cancel it in Google Play. See Delete Your Account.

Your rights

You can access, export, correct and delete your information in the app or by emailing us. If you are in the EU, UK or a similar region, you may also object to or restrict some processing, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and our database provider encrypts stored data. No system is perfectly secure, so please protect your sign-in.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.

Contact us

Questions or requests: support@serevo.org. We aim to reply within 48 hours.

Delete Your Account

Effective: October 6, 2026 · Last updated: October 6, 2026

You can delete your Serevo account and the data linked to it at any time.

In the app (fastest)

Open Serevo, go to Settings, then Data and Privacy, and choose Delete account. You can export your data there first. Deletion happens straight away.

By email

If you cannot open the app, email support@serevo.org from the email address on your account with the subject "Account Deletion Request". We will complete the request within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account and the data linked to it: profile, moods and notes, journal entries, intentions, chat history and chat summaries, activity and progress data, and subscription status in our database. The app's data on your phone is also removed.

What is not deleted straight away

  • Your Premium subscription is not cancelled. Cancel it in Google Play.
  • RevenueCat, our subscription provider, keeps its own customer record.
  • Backups at our hosting provider, and Anthropic's short retention period (about 30 days), may keep copies for a limited time.
  • Anything we must keep by law.
