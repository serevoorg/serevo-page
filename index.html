The practice

Simple by design

No pressure to perform. Just a quiet rhythm you can return to any time.

i

Open Serevo, any time

Morning, lunch break, before bed — whenever you have two minutes and want to return to yourself.

ii

Choose your practice

Breathe, write, check in with your mood, or set an intention. Each is a self-contained moment — use one or all four.

iii

Watch patterns emerge

Over days and weeks, your mood timeline and journal history quietly reveal what's working. Awareness you can act on.

iv

Make it a ritual

Consistency beats intensity. Even two minutes daily reshapes how you relate to your own mind. Serevo grows with you.

Privacy Policy

Effective: January 29, 2026 · Last updated: January 29, 2026

At Serevo, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, username, and profile picture you provide during registration
  • Health Data: Health metrics, mood tracking, goals, and activities you log in the app
  • Usage Data: App interactions, features used, and time spent within the application
  • Device Information: Device type, OS, unique identifiers, and mobile network information

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • To personalize your experience and provide tailored content
  • To communicate with you about updates, features, and support
  • To analyze usage patterns and optimize app performance
  • To send you notifications and reminders (with your consent)
  • To ensure security and prevent fraud or abuse

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances: with your explicit consent, to comply with legal obligations, to protect our rights and safety, or with service providers who assist in operating the app.

Your Data Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time through your account settings or by contacting us.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion, we will delete your data within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Serevo is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
